OUR SMILE PHILOSOPHY - THE ART OF PERSONALISED SMILE DESIGN

Ultimate Smile Design (USD) is a distinct, premium smile design concept and innovation of Advance Dental Export (ADE), a global name in advanced dentistry solutions headquartered in Surat, India. Born from a vision to elevate smile design, USD brings together personalisation, artistry, precision and the science of creating bespoke smiles.

Rooted in a dedication to understanding every smile's individuality, our approach considers facial harmony, proportions and natural expression to envision smiles that complement each person's unique identity.

Backed by ADE's expertise and a team of unparalleled Ultimate Smile Design-authorised smile design dentists, we bring clinical vision and laboratory craftsmanship together to create a more personalised smile design experience - helping patients feel confident that their smile reflects who they are. This commitment to excellence defines our pursuit of distinction in premium smile design.

FAQS

Frequently asked question

Can I explore ultimate smile design if I only want to make a small change to my smile?

Absolutely. Ultimate Smile Design doesn't have to mean a dramatic transformation. Whether you have a specific concern or simply want to explore subtle improvements, a consultation with one of our skilled and experienced Ultimate Smile Designers can help determine what is appropriate for your smile and personal goals.

What aspects of my smile can be improved through smile design?

Smile design can address concerns such as tooth shape, size, colour, spacing, alignment, proportions, uneven gum lines, excessive gum display, worn, chipped or missing teeth, and the relationship between your teeth, lips and facial features. Our authorised Ultimate Smile Designers will assess your smile, understand your concerns and recommend suitable treatments based on your oral health, individual needs and desired outcome.

Will my new smile look natural, or will it look noticeably different?

A natural-looking smile should complement your facial features, expressions and individual characteristics. At Ultimate Smile Design, our personalised approach focuses on creating a smile that feels harmonious with you, rather than recreating a standard look.

Do I need veneers to improve my smile?

Not necessarily. Veneers are just one of several smile design treatment options. Depending on your concerns and oral health, our authorised Ultimate Smile Designers may recommend alternatives such as teeth whitening, orthodontic treatment, dental bonding or other procedures. The right approach is personalised to your needs, and not every smile requires extensive treatment.

How long does the ultimate smile design process take?

The timeline depends on your personalised treatment plan, the procedures involved and your individual dental needs. While some treatments may be completed in a few appointments, others may require more time. Your Ultimate Smile Designer will explain the expected timeline after assessing your smile and discussing your goals.

How long will the results of my smile design last?

The longevity of your smile design results depends on the treatments, materials used, your oral health and aftercare routine. At Ultimate Smile Design, we use some of the finest-quality dental materials, carefully selected and crafted at Advance Dental Export (ADE), the lab behind Ultimate Smile Design. Regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene and following your Ultimate Smile Designer's aftercare advice can help maintain your results for years to come.

How do I find an Ultimate Smile Design dentist near me?

You can visit the Find a Dentist section on the Ultimate Smile Design website to search for our Smile Designers, hospitals or clinics in your city. Explore the available authorised Ultimate Smile Designers, choose one and request a consultation directly. Alternatively, fill out the contact form on our website for our team to reach out to you, or call us at +91 84698 88877.

How much does a smile makeover cost?

The cost of a smile makeover depends on your individual needs, the procedures recommended, the complexity of your case and the materials selected. Any underlying dental concerns that require attention may also influence the overall cost and treatment timeline.

After assessing your smile and understanding your expectations, your authorised Ultimate Smile Designer will explain the recommended treatment plan and provide a personalised cost breakdown. Throughout the process, our Smile Designer will guide you at every step, helping you make informed decisions with confidence.

What if I am not sure what needs to be changed about my smile?

You do not need to have a complete treatment plan in mind before your consultation. You can simply share what you feel about your smile, what you would like to improve or even what you are unsure about. Your Ultimate Smile Designer can evaluate your smile, understand your expectations and discuss suitable possibilities with you.

Can I get an Ultimate Smile Design before my wedding or a special occasion?

Absolutely! In fact, planning your smile design ahead of an important occasion is a good idea. We recommend scheduling a consultation well in advance to allow enough time for assessment and treatment. The timeline depends on your dental needs and the procedures involved. Your authorised Ultimate Smile Designer can help you understand what can realistically be achieved within your available time and guide you through the process.

What makes an Ultimate Smile Designer different from a regular cosmetic dentist?

An Ultimate Smile Designer follows a specialised, comprehensive approach that goes beyond conventional cosmetic dentistry. Every smile is evaluated through facial harmony, proportions, smile dynamics, tooth characteristics and individual expressions. Combining clinical expertise, digital smile design, advanced technology and the artistry of skilled dental technicians, an Ultimate Smile Designer follows a structured, personalised protocol to plan and craft smiles that complement each individual's features, preferences and personality.
