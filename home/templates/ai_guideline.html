How to Record Video for
Exocad TruSmile

Record your patient's video by following the steps below. A correctly captured video provides the facial data required for precise analysis in Exocad TruSmile.

7 Steps for Video Recording on Mobile

Follow these 7 steps for a smooth and efficient video recording. Additional guidance is available in the detailed instructions.

01

Camera settings

Set your mobile camera to 1080p (Full HD) resolution at 30 FPS before recording.

02

Flawless Lighting

Ensure the patient's face is well lit, and use a ring light whenever available.

03

Setup & Stability

Mount your phone on a tripod and use a plain wall for a stable background setup.

04

Elegant Framing

Record in portrait mode and keep the patient's teeth at the centre of the frame.

05

Camera Angle

Point the camera directly towards the patient's face and focus on the smile.

06

Video length

Keep the recording within 30 seconds or trim it before submitting the video.

07

Natural Expressions

Ask the patient to talk, smile naturally, and keep teeth visible during the video.

Please upload the video as the original file, document or an attachment. Avoid sending it through messaging apps like WhatsApp, as they often compress videos and reduce their quality.

Guidelines for Patient Actions

Follow these patient action guidelines to capture a stable and accurate video.

Preparation

Ask the patient to sit comfortably and remain steady with both hands resting on their lap.

During the Recording

Encourage the patient to perform the following actions throughout the recording.

Face directly towards the camera view

Perform facial movements steadily

Speak naturally throughout the video

Smile and laugh naturally and openly

Keep their teeth clearly visible

Avoid These Actions

Ensure the patient avoids the following actions throughout the recording.

Rapid head movements during the video

Moving out of the camera frame

Biting the lips during recording

Protruding the tongue during the video

Covering the mouth with the hands

Turning head, hiding mouth corners

Perform the patient's intra-oral scan within one week of the video recording to ensure the recorded video accurately reflects the patient's current dentition. Use only scans of teeth that have not been prepped.

This tool is intended exclusively for patients aged 18 years or older and should not be used for patients with underbites or supernumerary teeth.

Background & Environmental Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to create an ideal recording environment.

Tripod

Mount your device on a tripod to keep the recording steady and stable throughout the video.

Background

Use a plain, solid-coloured background free from patterns and distractions to ensure a clear video.

Lighting

Ensure optimal lighting using a ring light or another suitable light source. Position the light in front of the patient, preferably from the camera's direction, rather than behind or above them. Provide sufficient illumination to keep the anterior teeth clearly visible throughout the recording.

Video Orientation

Record the video in portrait mode. Landscape videos can be uploaded but are not recommended, especially for dual-view presentations. If a portrait video appears rotated, correct its orientation during the upload process.

Face Position

Position the patient's head in the upper portion of the frame, with the teeth approximately centred. Leave enough space for natural movement without making the face too small, as on-screen overlays may obstruct the teeth.

Avoid Camera Zoom

Do not use digital or optical zoom during recording, as it can reduce video quality and create an unstable perspective. Instead, move the camera closer and adjust the framing before you begin recording.

Camera Position

Position the camera directly in front of the patient's face, aimed at the nose. Record the patient from a frontal view with the full face visible in an upright posture, and keep the camera aligned at the same level as the patient's smile, avoiding both high and low angles.

Video Recording Requirements

These are recommended video recording requirements to capture high-quality patient footage suitable for Exocad TruSmile.

Video Length: 30s

Keep the video within 30 seconds. Record longer clips if needed and trim the best section using a video editor.

Video Resolution: 1080p to 4096p

Use a resolution between 1080p and 4096p. Full HD (1080p) is recommended to reduce upload time.

Frame Rate: 24 to 60 FPS

Select a frame rate between 24 and 60 FPS. Use 30 FPS for optimal recording quality and performance.

File Formats: Media Format

Upload videos in .mov, .mp4, .avi, or .mkv formats. Convert other formats before uploading if required.

Maximum Size: Below 700mb

Ensure the video file size remains below 700 MB. The size limit is usually maintained with a 30-second video.

Encoding Standards: Default

Use AVC (H.264), HEVC (H.265), MPEG-2, or MPEG-4 encoding formats. Smartphones support these by default.

Settings for iPhone Users

Use the recommended iPhone settings below to ensure accurate video capture and optimal processing.

Most Compatible Format

Select the “Most Compatible” format to save videos in the widely supported .mp4 format. Follow these steps to set it as the default.

1

Open Settings on your iPhone.

2

Go to Camera > Formats > Camera Capture.

3

Select the Most Compatible option.

High Efficiency Format

When using the High Efficiency format, follow these steps while downloading from iCloud.

1

Click on the three dots (...) menu.

2

Select More Download Options.

3

Choose Original Version or Highest Resolution to maintain the best video quality.

4

Open that video in Clipchamp & export it as .mp4 in Full HD resolution.
